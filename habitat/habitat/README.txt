HABITAT — Free Interior Design & Furniture Studio Template (by uiCookies)

Concept: An editorial, architectural single-page site for a fictional Copenhagen interior-design and furniture studio. It deliberately avoids the cream + serif + terracotta cliché of the niche: the identity leans on a deep loden green and brushed brass over a warm putty (green-grey) canvas, so it reads like a real high-end studio — oak, wool, honed stone and brass hardware — rather than an AI default. The layout idea is a room-by-room "catalogue": numbered projects, hairline dividers, generous whitespace, mostly square corners for an architectural feel, and a full-bleed hero whose serif headline sits over the interior shot.

Palette:
  Putty (canvas)      #E9E3D8
  Paper (surfaces)    #F4F0E8
  Espresso ink (text) #23211C
  Loden green (brand) #37432F  / deep #29321F
  Brushed brass       #A6803E  / soft #C6A56A
  Taupe (meta/borders)#7A7465

Font pairing:
  Display — Cormorant Garamond (elegant high-contrast serif, used large for headings and quotes)
  Body/UI — Hanken Grotesk (warm humanist sans for copy, labels and buttons; uppercase letter-spaced eyebrows)

Build: Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted in css/vendor + js/vendor), custom design in css/style.css, all interactions in vanilla js/main.js (mobile nav, portfolio room-filter, testimonial slider, furniture scroller, form validation, IntersectionObserver scroll-reveal). Icons are inline SVG only. Real local images in /img. Responsive 360px–1440px, accessible (skip link, landmarks, focus-visible), and respects prefers-reduced-motion.
