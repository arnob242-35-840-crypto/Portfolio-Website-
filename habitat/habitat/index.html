The studio

A room should hold the way you actually live — slow mornings, long dinners, quiet corners — and still feel considered ten years on.

Habitat is a fifteen-person studio working across residential interiors, renovations and bespoke furniture. We take on a small number of projects each year so every home gets the attention of a principal designer, start to finish.

What we do

Design that runs the whole way through

From first floor plan to the last cushion, we stay hands-on. Take the full scope or the single room you're stuck on.

01

Full-Home Interiors

End-to-end design for a whole house or apartment — layout, materials, lighting, furniture and styling under one plan.

02

Kitchen & Bath

The rooms that earn their keep. Bespoke cabinetry, honest stone, and fittings specified to last a couple of decades.

03

Furniture & Joinery

Custom pieces built by our workshop — sofas, tables, wardrobes and shelving cut to your rooms, not a catalogue.

04

Space Planning

Reworking how a home flows — moving walls, borrowing light and rethinking circulation before a single finish is chosen.

05

Styling & Art

The finishing layer — textiles, ceramics, books and curated art placed room by room on install day.

06

Colour & Material

A standalone consult for the palette and finishes — swatches, samples and a schedule you can hand to any trade.

How we work

Four steps, one point of contact

Every project runs on the same clear path — no jargon, no surprise line items, and the same designer from the first visit to the final styling.

01

Consultation

We visit your space, listen to how you live, and map the brief, budget and timeline together.

02

Concept & Design

Mood, materials, floor plans and photoreal 3D visuals so you can walk the home before we build it.

03

Sourcing & Making

We commission the furniture, order every finish, and coordinate the trades on site day to day.

04

Install & Styling

Delivery, installation and the final layer of styling — you walk in to a finished, photographed home.

The designer

Led by Marit Sørensen

Marit trained as an architect before turning to interiors, and it shows in how Habitat works — we start with light and structure, then let the softer layers follow. After fifteen years and more than two hundred homes, the studio still takes on only a handful of projects at a time.

"A good room isn't styled — it's understood. We design for the life that will actually happen inside it."
Marit Sørensen
Founder & Principal Designer
